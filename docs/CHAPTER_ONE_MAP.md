# Chapter One Map

## Title

The Bell Beneath the Rain

## Chapter promise

Maelin must escape execution and learn why the supposedly dead king arranged her imprisonment.

## Answer delivered

The king imprisoned Maelin because she failed at an earlier, erased attempt to contain the crown prison. Her crime and part of her identity were cut out of history.

## Question carried forward

Who is the second Maelin, and what happened during the seven failed versions of the king's murder?

## Major route flow

### Opening cell

The player escapes through strength, deception, or contact with the Veil. This establishes a preferred method and determines what Maelin first learns about her altered identity.

### Brann's crisis

The player saves or abandons the gaoler. Brann later appears alive with evidence or dead at an impossible age. The consequence proves that convergence does not erase responsibility.

### Hidden passage

The player gains a weapon, the prison ledger, a vision of six other Witnesses, or a memory of Maelin committing the royal murder.

### Crown door

A visible stat check lets the player use force, spend a Cinder, or discover the eighth lock through Guile.

### Hollow Vault

The king reveals that Maelin's failure matters more than the murder charge. The player renews duty, reaches for power, or breaks the ancient law.

## Endings

### An Oath With Teeth

Maelin protects the king and meets Rook during the first open attack from the pit. This route centers duty and uneasy alliance.

### The Crown Remembers

Maelin touches the crown and sees Rook kill her in a possible future. This route centers power, fate, and mistrust.

### The Eighth Prisoner

Maelin cuts a chain and releases the missing half of herself. This route centers identity, forbidden truth, and the strongest direct cliffhanger.

## State carried to Chapter Two

* Brann saved, abandoned, or burned
* Thornwake recovered or still doubled
* secret ledger held or absent
* portrait warning kept, burned, or unseen
* memory double accepted or denied
* crown seal opened by force, blood, or the eighth lock
* final stance toward oath, power, or rebellion
* remaining Vitality, Resolve, Guile, Mercy, and Cinders
