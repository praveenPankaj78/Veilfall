# Chapter Four Map

## Thief at the Mileless Bridge

Playable lead: Caelan Vey

Visiting lead: Rook Sable

Target length: fifteen decisions on every route

Primary objective: recover the World Nail fragment and keep Caelan's people alive while Crown soldiers destroy the stable bridge.

Force identity: the soldiers on the bridge are the surviving part of Ordan's covert Asterra detachment. They wear the Crown badge of Caelan's own kingdom. At the end of the chapter, their willingness to abandon Ordan and Regent Malrec's genuine order prove that authority above Ordan is directing the recovery.

Chapter answer: the fragment contains a map of nine World Nails. The northern mark at Dragonspine is already active.

Next hook: Regent Malrec has issued a genuine order sending the fragment to Dragonspine. Caelan does not yet know whether the order is protection, a trap, or both.

## Imported Chapter Three outcomes

### Caelan pursued Ordan

Caelan begins close to Ordan and can see the courier reach for the fragment. Rook still takes it first.

### Caelan pursued the thief

Caelan begins close to Rook. The mirrored coin from Harrowfen confirms that this is the same prepared thief the player saw in the market.

### Caelan secured the return road

The whole group enters together through Lysara's guide thread. Ordan and Rook have a longer lead, but Harrowfen remains easier to reach.

These openings change advantage and feeling without changing the clear immediate goal.

## Main route

1. Enter the Mileless Bridge and decide whether to protect the group, close the distance, anchor Harrowfen, or preserve resources.
2. Survive a Crown volley while tracking Rook and Ordan.
3. Corner Rook and learn that he wants the map inside the fragment.
4. React when Crown soldiers cut the Bell Arch loose.
5. Decide how to protect the injured Brann without losing the pursuit.
6. Choose one of three distinct spans.
7. Cross the chosen environment and gain its route advantage.
8. Use or limit Rook's false Crown officer performance.
9. Decide whether Ordan is worth saving as a witness.
10. Open the fragment and discover the map of nine Nails.
11. Share a private decision with Mara about law, duty, honesty, and attraction.
12. Break the Crown force surrounding the Map Arch.
13. Turn Rook's travelling theatre into a believable escape.
14. Hold the final road until every survivor crosses.
15. Read Regent Malrec's order and decide whether to arrest, bargain with, or trust Rook.

## Environmental routes

### Mountain span

The player faces cold, an exposed climb, and blue flames that answer an Oath. The open snow makes enemies easy to see.

### Storm span

The player faces waves, poor footing, and hidden shooters. The storm conceals the group from crossbows.

### Brass span

The player crosses moving road plates inside a huge underground machine. The wheels can also cut off pursuit.

Every route returns to the Crown Span after one decision. This gives the player a strong sense of travel without creating three separate chapter plots.

## Rook's surprise contract

Rook's large tricks use objects shown before the result:

- reversible black and royal blue coat;
- black wax;
- silver wire;
- mirrored coins;
- flash salt;
- voice changing reeds;
- a compact travelling theatre curtain.

His first large surprise impersonates Ordan and sends soldiers onto the wrong span. His second turns the same tools into false officers and false escape routes.

The player sees the ingredients first but should not predict the complete performance. Rook may cooperate, but he never becomes obedient. His humour stops during Brann's injury and returns only when the immediate human danger has passed.

## Caelan's ownership of the crossover

Caelan owns every duty decision:

- who receives protection during the collapse;
- what happens to Ordan;
- who may read the map;
- how the final road is held;
- what legal or practical relationship he chooses with Rook.

Rook owns theft, deception, strange routes, and his final escape with a copy. Neither hero can be exchanged for the other without changing the scene.

## Romance movement

Mara's scene comes after the map reveal and before the final assault. The danger remains immediate, so the scene is brief but emotionally direct.

The player can:

- admit that law must bend when it stops protecting people;
- bind Caelan to personal honesty through an Oath;
- kiss Mara if enough Trust and Attraction have been earned;
- protect the relationship without making a promise during a crisis.

Mara's judgment continues into the final Rook decision. The scene does not pause the plot or turn danger into an excuse for intimacy.

## Endings

### Arrest

Caelan applies the law and takes Rook toward Dragonspine as a prisoner. Rook escapes the closed cuff but continues north. Caelan keeps the fragment.

### Bargain

Rook names the buyer's meeting arrangement and agrees to guide Caelan north. Their cooperation has explicit terms. Caelan keeps the fragment.

### Trust

Caelan places the survivors in Rook's hands and lets him choose his own road. Rook reveals a safe exit and accepts a personal debt. Caelan keeps the fragment.

All three endings award the same Wayfire. The choice expresses duty and relationship instead of rewarding one answer with a stronger total gain.

## Continuity rules for later chapters

- Rook always leaves with at least a partial map copy.
- Caelan always keeps the real World Nail fragment.
- Ordan's survival and custody depend on the bridge choice.
- Sacrificing gear at the anchor removes part of the documentary case against the Crown.
- Allowing a full wax copy gives Rook more accurate knowledge and creates a debt.
- Mara remembers Caelan's stated rule about law, any honesty Oath, and any kiss.
- Chapter Five must explain how the group reaches Dragonspine and why cold blue fire matters before offering choices that use it.
