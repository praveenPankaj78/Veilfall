# Character and Series Bible

## Cast rules

There are exactly three playable protagonists: two men and one woman. All protagonists and all romance interests are adults aged twenty five or older. Each lead has a distinct method, cost, voice, moral pressure, travel route, and finale.

Romances are heterosexual. A recurring relationship can deepen across a season, while some attractions last only for one region or one dangerous bargain. Intimacy may carry hidden motives or political leverage, but physical consent remains voluntary. A violation can exist as villainy in the world, but it is never presented as romance or a reward.

# Caelan Vey

## Identity

- Gender: male
- Age at start: 31
- People: human
- Class: Oathwarden
- Series: The Ember Oath
- Starting place: Greyhaven, Crownlands
- Final place: the Black Gate and the Cinder Deep

## Background

Caelan grew up at a roadside inn run by his widowed father. He learned early that travellers remember the person who stands between them and danger. At seventeen he joined the King's Road Wardens after raiders killed a merchant family he had promised to guide safely. He rose through discipline, courage, and an unusual gift for making magic answer to spoken vows.

He is respected, physically capable, and comfortable giving orders. He is also too willing to carry blame for everyone near him. His deepest fear is not death. It is surviving after a promise fails.

## How he solves problems

Caelan deals with danger openly. He protects the vulnerable point, reads terrain, names a priority, and commits. In conversation he prefers a hard truth to an elegant lie. His best choices feel like leadership rather than simple aggression.

Primary actions:

- Guard: intercept harm aimed at another person.
- Command: turn trust and position into coordinated action.
- Breakline: use force to create a route through danger.
- Read the field: identify the threat that matters most.
- Swear: accept a binding duty to gain Oathfire.

## Power and cost

- Stamina powers combat, pursuit, climbing, and physical protection.
- Resolve resists fear, pain, manipulation, and despair.
- Oathfire enables mythic feats such as holding a burning gate or cutting a devil's contract.
- Every active Oath has a clear condition. Breaking it causes an Oathscar, removes an ability, damages trust, and changes later choices.

Growth path: road captain, ember bearer, dragon sworn guardian, then possible Gatekeeper or Oathbreaker King.

## Voice

Caelan notices exits, hands, weather, tired people, and who has been left unprotected. His humour is dry and usually arrives when he is frightened. He does not describe himself as noble.

## Central relationships

### Mara Renn

Mara is a 29 year old human royal scout, Caelan's oldest friend, and the recurring emotional anchor of the first series. She is quick eyed, athletic, frank, and more comfortable in motion than in court clothes. Attraction grows through familiar teasing, shared night watches, jealousy neither enjoys admitting, and disagreements about duty.

Possible path: trusted friend, lover, committed partner, or painful separation. If the relationship becomes sexual, the scene must reveal a choice about the road ahead rather than pause the plot.

### Lysara Quill

Lysara is a 36 year old elven envoy from Thornweald. She moves with deliberate grace, wears living silk that changes with her pulse, and listens so closely that attention itself feels intimate. She wants Caelan to reject human control of the Nails.

Possible path: political flirtation, brief consensual affair, genuine respect, or elegant betrayal. She may sincerely desire him while still using proximity to learn his Oaths. Caelan can recognise the manipulation and consent to the encounter on his own terms, refuse it, or turn the leverage back on her.

### Vexa Ash

Vexa is an adult devil advocate whose chosen appearance is that of a striking woman in her thirties. Her beauty is precise rather than soft: a calm mouth, ember bright eyes, and clothing that seems cut from shadow at the instant before flame. She bargains for access to Caelan's true name.

Possible path: dangerous flirtation, a negotiated intimate encounter, adversarial respect, or permanent enmity. Every condition is spoken before physical intimacy. Her hidden motive may be larger than the stated price, but she cannot remove Caelan's ability to choose.

## Character arc

Caelan begins by believing a good man keeps every promise. He ends by understanding that no promise is moral merely because it was sworn. His strongest form is not a man carrying infinite duties. It is a leader who chooses which duties deserve power and lets others choose beside him.

# Rook Sable

## Identity

- Gender: male
- Age at start: 27
- People: human
- Class: thief and sensory illusionist
- Series: A Thief of Dawn
- Starting place: Greyhaven's river roofs
- Final place: the Luminous Court

## Background

Rook was raised in a travelling theatre that used sleight of hand, disguises, and minor magic. When the Crown accused the troupe of spying, his parents vanished into a labour prison and his younger sister Senna was taken into royal service. Rook survived by turning stagecraft into theft.

He says he wants money and freedom. In truth, he wants proof that nobody can own him again. He changes names often and leaves relationships before affection becomes a handle.

## How he solves problems

Rook avoids the strongest part of a problem. He observes routines, creates one believable distraction, and moves through the space people stop watching. In conversation he lies with truth, letting someone reach the wrong conclusion without making a claim he must later defend.

Primary actions:

- Slip: cross a watched space through timing and agility.
- Palm: move, plant, or replace a small object unnoticed.
- Mimic: reproduce a voice, posture, or social role after study.
- Glimmer: borrow a sight or sound from a nearby possible event.
- Vanish: spend every advantage to end pursuit, usually abandoning something valuable.

## Power and cost

- Dexterity powers climbing, locks, escapes, and precise theft.
- Focus maintains disguises, calculations, and Glimmers.
- Heat measures how strongly factions are searching for him.
- Glimmers borrow unrealised possibilities from the Unsea. Heavy use creates Echoes, brief moments when Rook remembers a life he never lived.

Growth path: rooftop thief, impossible burglar, walker of unreal roads, then thief of a law that governs the world.

## Voice

Rook notices value, weight, windows, clothing, insecurity, and the rhythm of attention. His jokes are fast and often hide a precise judgment. When he speaks without wit, the moment matters.

## Central relationships

### Sen Aldis

Sen is a 30 year old human smuggler and former stage dancer. She has a warm, commanding presence, strong legs earned on rigging and rooftops, and a smile that often arrives before a dangerous proposal. She knows Rook's oldest name and dislikes being treated as a safe place he can visit without giving anything back.

Possible path: recurring lover, committed partner, trusted accomplice, or rival captain. Their physical familiarity can precede emotional honesty. The relationship grows only when Rook stops using charm to avoid direct answers.

### Nyra Venn

Nyra is a 28 year old goblin pressure engineer in Brassreach. She is compact, copper skinned, sharply dressed, and happiest when grease marks one cheek and a room full of officials has underestimated her. She wants Rook to steal her own invention before the Compact turns it into a weapon.

Possible path: playful flirtation, brief affair, technical partnership, or hostile competition. Their attraction grows from cleverness and shared risk. She leaves the continuing story after Brassreach unless the player accepts a costly later favour.

### Aveline of the Seventh Bell

Aveline is an adult female celestial who looks human but casts no shadow. Her stillness makes every small motion noticeable. She studies desire as a dangerous form of uncertainty and initially approaches Rook to learn how mortals choose what they know will hurt.

Possible path: intellectual seduction, consensual intimacy with conflicting motives, mercy, or betrayal. Rook can teach her uncertainty while she can tempt him with a life in which nobody he loves can leave.

## Character arc

Rook begins by treating escape as victory. He ends with the power to steal a law from reality and must decide what he is willing to belong to afterward. His best ending does not tame him. It makes his freedom something he can share.

# Ilyra Fen

## Identity

- Gender: female
- Age at start: 34
- People: half elf
- Class: memory witch and root mage
- Series: The Worldroot Witch
- Starting place: the Shard Coast
- Final place: Worldroot

## Background

Ilyra was born to an elven root singer and a human storm captain. She grew up between cultures that each believed the other careless with memory. When a childhood storm destroyed her island, she saved several neighbours with a spell she cannot remember learning. The spell cost every memory of her father.

She became a forensic witch to learn where magic ends and self deception begins. She is composed, observant, and capable of frightening decisions when evidence supports them. Her private fear is that her personality is only the shape left by things she has forgotten.

## How she solves problems

Ilyra names the hidden rule, prepares the correct spell, and changes one relationship at a time. She is strongest with time, evidence, and a willing ritual circle. When surprised, she can force magic quickly by paying with memory or bodily Strain.

Primary actions:

- Threadread: see the emotional or magical connection between people and objects.
- Ward: define what may cross a boundary and under which condition.
- Glassform: store a memory, spell, or sensation in prepared glass.
- Rootcall: ask a living place what changed within it.
- Rewrite: alter one magical rule at a severe named cost.

## Power and cost

- Mana powers prepared spells and returns through rest, food, ritual, and safe connection.
- Memory Shards are specific personal memories stored as emergency power. Spending one changes later narration, relationships, or available knowledge.
- Strain rises when Ilyra forces magic. High Strain causes sensory mistakes and unreliable options that are signalled to the player.
- Names give spells precision. Using a true name without permission creates a lasting corruption mark.

Growth path: forensic witch, storm binder, realm walker, Worldroot author.

## Voice

Ilyra notices patterns, smells, changes in light, exact wording, living things, and what a speaker avoids. Her wit is patient and unexpectedly sharp. Desire makes her more direct, not less intelligent.

## Central relationships

### Tomas Venn

Tomas is a 37 year old human historian and travelling printer. He has a broad, ink stained build, an easy laugh, and the habit of rolling his sleeves before an argument. He believes truth should be public. Ilyra believes some truth belongs to the person who survived it.

Possible path: intellectual flirtation, lovers, lasting partnership, or principled separation. Their intimacy works best after a disagreement is resolved through respect rather than surrender.

### Caelan Vey

An optional slow relationship can begin when Ilyra and Caelan cross on the Ember Steppe. She respects that he acts while afraid. He respects that she names costs others hide. Attraction grows through shared command and quiet care after battle.

This path requires both characters to be unattached when imported decisions are used. It can culminate in intimacy before Caelan enters the Cinder Deep, making his separate finale more personal without changing Ilyra's own final agency.

### Rook Sable

An optional volatile relationship can begin in Serekh or the Unsea. Ilyra sees through his performance, while Rook makes her laugh when certainty becomes heavy. Their magic interacts dangerously because his Glimmers offer memories she might have lived.

This path is mutually exclusive with other committed paths. A physical relationship may begin before either trusts the other, but it develops only if Rook does not steal her memories and Ilyra does not read him without permission.

### Korran Red Wind

Korran is a 39 year old male orc wind caller and seasonal leader on the Ember Steppe. He is tall, scarred, patient, and openly appreciative without assuming ownership. He wants Ilyra to stop the dead from directing the living, even if it means losing his mother's returned voice.

Possible path: respectful flirtation, a brief intimate bond during the steppe campaign, political alliance, or moral opposition. He remains important to the regional outcome even if romance ends.

## Character arc

Ilyra begins by believing enough knowledge can prevent regret. She ends facing a choice no complete evidence can settle. Her growth is learning that uncertainty does not excuse inaction and that memory is part of a person, not the whole of one.

# Shared relationship rules

1. Attraction never replaces the chapter objective.
2. Flirtation should reveal competence, fear, values, or leverage.
3. Temporary lovers can leave after their regional role is complete. Their departure should still change information, trust, access, or emotional tone.
4. A hidden political or personal motive does not erase consent to physical intimacy. Magic, threats, intoxication, or false consent do.
5. An intimate scene changes at least one story state: trust, debt, vulnerability, allegiance, information, or future danger.
6. Physical description stays selective and character based. Use movement, fabric, expression, warmth, scent, strength, and the protagonist's attention. Do not inventory anatomy.
7. Jealousy can create tension but never turns a love interest into property.
8. Each protagonist can finish fulfilled without a permanent romance.

# Intersection rules

- Caelan and Rook first cross in Chapter Three of Caelan's series.
- Ilyra meets Caelan on the Ember Steppe after his road has become a public cause.
- Rook and Ilyra cross in Serekh and again in the Unsea.
- No intersection repeats the same scenes or objective from both viewpoints.
- Imported decisions appear as changed circumstances, not a long summary of another series.
- The protagonist whose series is being played retains the decisive action in every crossover.
