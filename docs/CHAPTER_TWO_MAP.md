# Chapter Two Map

## Status

This chapter is implemented and playable after any Chapter One ending. Its full narration pass is complete: all active scenes, route callbacks, choice results, endings, and journal entries use the continuous close second person style.

## Chapter

Series: The Ember Oath

Lead: Caelan Vey

Title: The Inn That Waited

Primary objective: protect the wounded during the siege of Bellweather Inn, then drive the loosened road pin back into place.

Primary mystery: who prepared the inn as a trap, and why do the attackers want the buried iron?

Player facing threat rhythm: Immediate during arrival, Rising while treating the wounded and investigating, Uneasy during one private watch, Critical during the siege and tunnel fight, then Rising when Ordan's trail points toward Harrowfen.

## Imported consequences

The opening remembers how Caelan crossed the broken road, whether the treaty chest survived, whether an attacker was captured, who was wounded, current relationships, active Oaths, evidence, and remaining resources. The silver road rises from the flood, the high ground route descends from Rainwatch Hill, and the Oath route follows a roped line along the shallow road beneath the impossible sea. One sealed dose of Medicine survives.

Choice results enter the following scene as physical action rather than a detached recap. Caelan's thoughts appear through breath, touch, trained habits, interrupted movement, and what he cannot stop looking at. Explanations remain in dialogue or visible cause and effect whenever possible.

## Opening movement

A pale mire hound follows the bleeding escort through the flood. It has a ridged pale body, two long front legs, and two low red eyes. It follows the scent of blood. Maelin Bellweather gets the group inside and reveals that Warden Jory arrived three days earlier with an arrow in his back and a forged warning in Caelan's handwriting.

Maelin still lights a room for Dain, her husband, who vanished through the stable door during the first road disturbance three weeks earlier. Her wait gives the inn its title and makes closing the displaced road personal.

Jory used a distant shortcut that had been pulled beside the inn. At this stage, the player learns only the strange rule needed to understand the danger: distant road ends are being forced beside Bellweather. Distance is changing, not time. Earlier crews could pull one doorway open for a few seconds with hooks and chains. They could not move a whole road. The road pin, its two locks, and Ordan's need for Caelan and Lysara are revealed only when the player reaches the buried chamber.

## Medicine choice

The final dose can go to Lysara, Nilo, or Garran. Garran is either the road prisoner or an attacker Maelin found emerging from the cellar, depending on the earlier route. Joren's side wound has been cleaned and stitched, so he is stable and does not need the sealed medicine. Nilo survives without the dose but may lose his leg. The living magic belongs to Lysara's glass seed. A living shard cut her palm when the seed cracked, and her hand guides that magic. Losing the hand would therefore cost her control of the seed and threaten the treaty. Garran may die with the strongest testimony against Ordan.

## Investigation routes

The player chooses one lead:

- Maelin's ledger links Ordan to rope, oil, hooks, and disguised royal soldiers.
- The cellar reveals the enemy rope, the iron latch that holds it, and the coastal road pulled against the old tunnel.
- Garran identifies Ordan and explains that the attackers plan to pull the pin free.

Each route supplies a distinct advantage during the same siege. Earlier preparation also matters. Searching the entrance earns a no-cost cellar search, finding the pantry break delays the mire hound, and studying the pin's surviving locking tooth earns the safer wagon method.

## Relationship movement

Before the attack, Caelan and Mara receive one quiet minute. They can name a fear, kiss when their existing bond supports it, choose a close friendship explicitly, or remain focused on duty. Friendship restores Resolve and gives the relationship a complete future rather than postponing romance.

## Siege and dungeon

Crown soldiers attack the front while the mire hound breaks through the pantry. Caelan protects the wounded, identifies the cellar as the true target, and chooses Mara, Lysara, or Maelin for the descent.

The cellar opens into an old road tunnel touching a distant beach and mountain pass. The player crosses a physical dungeon with enemy soldiers, unstable stone, and the pursuing mire hound. Mara reads the ambush, Lysara follows living thread, and Maelin reads the builders' support marks. The direct chain route reaches the chamber fastest but meets prepared soldiers. The iron latch opens a slower maintenance route around them.

## Mystery answer

The road pin is an ancient anchor that keeps road ends connected to the correct places. Route authority is the canonical term for a signed road order and its Warden seal together. One old lock responds to Caelan's route authority. The other responds to the living magic in Lysara's glass seed. Crossing Bellweather's old threshold stores both signatures, so the second lock remains active even when Lysara stays upstairs. Ordan sabotaged the treaty escort to bring Caelan and Lysara over the threshold, then wounded the group so sheltering at Bellweather became unavoidable. With both locks active, his chain can move whole road ends instead of one doorway.

Caelan reseats the pin through command, strength, a new binding Oath, or a wagon method. Brann and two guards take the enemy chain. Tivik removes an axle from the rear supply wagon. If the player studied the locking tooth, the axle seats the pin without risking Caelan or the treaty, but the wagon remains behind. Without that preparation, the group can sacrifice the supply wagon and some cargo. After the pin is seated, the distant roads close, the soldiers are separated, the mire hound follows blood through the closing gap, and the last soldier surrenders.

## Closing choice

Caelan witnesses Ordan tear a waxed map page from the chamber wall and escape through the eastern service passage. Every surviving paper, witness, and important object travels toward Harrowfen. The closing choice determines which proof Caelan presents first: ordinary testimony, the broken iron fragment, or a public Oath against the Crown plot. The fragment is visibly packed on every ending and still pulls toward Harrowfen, shortening the next journey to one mile.
