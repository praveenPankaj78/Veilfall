# Chapter Eleven Map: City of Every Price

## Identity and canonical entry

- Title: City of Every Price
- Series: The Ember Oath
- Playable hero: Caelan Vey
- Canonical entry: `c11-vathis-gate`
- Location: Vathis, a Cinder Deep city whose public streets, doors, shelter, and weather obey purchased contracts
- Opening physical state: Caelan, Vexa, the recovered Gate Nail fragment, the exact Chapter Ten expedition, and only the partner explicitly recorded as crossing stand at Vathis's public gate.
- Immediate objective: identify who controls access to Malrec's engine, then reach it before the invasion auction closes.
- Carry-over question: Which faction controls Vathis and Malrec's engine?
- Early answer: the Price Court controls Vathis's public contracts and the invasion auction. House Sableglass holds the Court's current engine-access lease. The Free Ledger and Ash Compact can contest that lease but cannot open the engine district alone.
- Central question: Why is Malrec willing to merge the realms?
- Chapter answer: Malrec remembers a daughter named Elian and believes merger can restore her. A voice identifying herself as Elian answers through the engine and asks Caelan to stop that method.
- Next hook: What new promise can replace the failing law of the Black Gate?

The Price Court is a civic bench of public contract judges. It is not a people or a devil house. Its three open seats validate street contracts, leases, and public sales. House Sableglass currently controls two seats through purchased obligations. The third seat hears challenges. This institution is introduced through its three physical seals at the gate before its name appears in a choice.

## Known, Suspected, and Unknown

### Known at entry

- A spoken desire can make an offer appear, but an offer owns nothing before clear acceptance.
- Benefit, price, duration, exit, and accepting act must be stated before a bargain binds.
- A completed bargain cannot silently widen.
- The Cinder Deep is politically divided. The Free Ledger and Ash Compact oppose a complete merger because separation protects individual refusal.
- Malrec has House Sableglass allies who collect and release promises to weaken Worldroot.
- Caelan carries one recovered Gate Nail fragment under bargain custody, admitted theft, or public evidence custody.
- True-name precision, when present, reaches only the witnessed Chapter Nine fragment alignment scope.
- The Chapter Ten expedition roster and partner crossing state are physical facts.

### Suspected at entry

- Three faction banners above the engine district reflect competing legal claims rather than equal physical control.
- Chapter Ten's shared ledger, private seals, or completed burden Oath may affect who can prove a claim in Vathis.
- The planned invasion auction may transfer access to the Black Gate as well as military permission.
- Malrec needs the engine for something more personal than conquest.

### Unknown at entry

- Malrec remembers a daughter named Elian.
- He believes merger can restore her.
- A voice identifying herself as Elian can answer through the engine.
- Whether that voice is a dead soul, the only Elian, the objectively correct Elian, or physically inside the engine.
- The later truth that multiple equally real Elians exist.
- The later Serekh and celestial-engine evidence.
- The promise that can replace the Black Gate's failing law.

## Five Vathis location anchors

- Silhouette: black towers shaped like open hands surround a white engine spire, while contract ribbons bridge the fingers.
- Motion: street stones slide into new routes when a paid passage begins and drop back into walls when its duration expires.
- Sound: small bells ring once for an offer, twice for acceptance, and once in reverse when a price expires.
- Social rule: a public object may offer service after hearing a want. Silence, fear, attraction, and curiosity accept nothing. Only the stated word or action binds.
- Danger: an expired price can remove the street, door, roof, or weather protection beneath a traveller. House Sableglass also hides listening ash in public contracts.

## Factions and engine control

### Price Court

The Price Court validates public contracts, maintains the moving streets, and runs the invasion auction. It wants Vathis's law to remain enforceable and profitable. House Sableglass owns obligations controlling two of its three seats.

### House Sableglass

Sableglass holds the current engine-access lease. It intends to buy the invasion right for Malrec, release collected promises through the engine, and preserve its own status during the merger. Public exposure, listening-ash proof, and attack evidence weaken its legal position.

### Free Ledger

The Free Ledger protects separate records, voluntary exits, and the right to refuse. It contains reformers, cautious witnesses, and debt-bound signers with different tolerances for revolt. Its guide bargain provides one checkpoint passage and outside identification of the engine district. Its petition hearing can establish standing for collective refusal or a lawful bid.

### Ash Compact

Vexa's Compact prefers witnessed passage between realms and opposes a merger that would end distinct contracts and people. It can provide foreign standing or a public truce. It will not erase an admitted theft, a refused relationship, or hostile terms for convenience.

## Invasion auction rules

The auction sells one invasion right, not ownership of Edrath. The valid holder may open the inner Black Gate once, send only the named registered force through it, use the purchased Vathis streets to reach that Gate, and remain in Edrath until the next Vathis bell. The right does not own mortal names, bodies, affection, sexual consent, private promises, or unlisted land. It cannot transfer the Gate fragment's neutral-custody return promise.

A bid must be an obligation the bidder owns and may legally transfer. Before each auction option, the player sees the obligation, owner, transfer status, immediate advantage, and future restriction.

Legitimate bid sources include:

- a Free Ledger hearing credit earned only after the promised public hearing;
- a public damages claim against Sableglass created by exposure evidence;
- an independent witness bond created from exact Futureless, Crown volunteer, or mixed-company testimony;
- an Ash Compact public surety offered only under a cooperative Vexa state;
- Caelan's own transferable Price Court appearance obligation, which restricts his later right to refuse a named hearing.

Affection, intimacy, true-name access, another person's offer, Vaor's permission, the burden Oath, Red Moot authority, Crown restitution, and the fragment return promise are never auction currency.

No save can dead-end. Weak evidence may require the limited appearance obligation or a slower proof challenge, but never an illegal sale.

## Mythic Oathfire rules

Vathis teaches the rule with a temporary rain-shelter contract before a decisive use. Ordinary Oathfire strengthens an act. Mythic Oathfire also moves one named freedom from Caelan to one eligible active promise. The promise then decides that freedom until the stated duration ends.

Every mythic choice displays:

- resource cost;
- exclusive immediate advantage;
- exact active promise receiving the freedom;
- exact freedom Caelan loses;
- duration and success condition;
- breach condition;
- whether the restriction reaches Chapter Twelve.

Planned uses:

1. Shelter demonstration, 1 Oathfire: the active bring-them-home Oath receives Caelan's right to leave the shelter before the last expedition member. It lasts until the ash rain stops and gains a protected dry crossing. Breach means leaving first. It ends in Chapter Eleven.
2. Revolt, 2 Oathfire: the surviving Red Moot authority Oath receives Caelan's right to issue an order outside each fighter's accepted command. It lasts until the inner Gate begins opening. It creates a protected refusal square. Breach burns the square and carries an Oathscar. The restriction reaches Chapter Twelve until the Gate law is replaced.
3. Auction, 2 Oathfire: the surviving Crown restitution Oath receives Caelan's right to refuse the Price Court hearing about hidden Concord victims. It lasts until those victims receive a lawful hearing. It makes their claim legally unerasable and wins an exclusive bid advantage. It carries into Chapter Twelve.
4. Force, 2 Oathfire: the surviving clan-refusal Oath receives Caelan's right to cross a contracted door before the last willing traveller. It lasts until the opening stops or everyone reaches a freely chosen safe side. It keeps a retreat door open for every consenting traveller. Crossing early breaches it. The restriction reaches Chapter Twelve while the Gate remains open.

Destroyed or released Oaths never appear as power, payment, or selectable mythic recipients. Every mythic scene has a complete non-mythic route.

## Revelation ladder

1. A street forms under a paid token and falls back into a wall when its marked duration ends.
2. The Free Ledger guide or slower public route reveals the three Price Court seals and the Sableglass engine lease.
3. A public hearing, street record, or independent investigation proves that Sableglass controls engine access while the Price Court controls the auction.
4. Debt-bound devils show how transferable service promises became work collars. Named workers disagree about risk and choose their own response.
5. The auction notice defines the invasion right and names Malrec's Sableglass bid.
6. The relationship illusion uses a real desire but offers no real person's consent or knowledge. Chapter Ten limits expose its false price.
7. Revolt records, auction audit language, or a captured force-route relay supplies the route-specific engine phrase needed to open a witnessed channel.
8. A voice identifying herself as Elian answers. The voice says Malrec remembers her as his daughter and believes merger can restore her. She asks Caelan to stop him from doing it this way.
9. The engine drives the inner Black Gate open. The immediate question becomes which new promise can replace its failing law.

## Decision structure

1. Enter through the promised Free Ledger checkpoint or take the slower public street.
2. If the petition is owed, hold the public hearing and choose how to protect its signers. If it is not owed, investigate the public seals without claiming a hearing credit.
3. Answer the first purchased street's expiring price.
4. Open a contract door through custody, evidence, map, or witnessed passage.
5. Accept or refuse the temporary weather shelter and, when eligible, test the first mythic freedom transfer.
6. Account privately to every burden-Oath participant or preserve private seals and shared-ledger limits.
7. Face the relationship or non-romantic illusion.
8. Inventory legal obligations and liabilities before the auction bell.
9. Choose revolt, auction, or force.
10. Prepare the selected route through its exact roster or proof source.
11. Choose whether to use a route-specific mythic act where an eligible Oath survives.
12. Resolve the route's civic, legal, or physical crisis.
13. Secure the route-specific engine phrase.
14. Reach and activate the witnessed engine channel.
15. Answer Elian without claiming certainty about what she is.
16. Reach the inner Black Gate and preserve the route's cost into its ending.

## Defining routes

### Revolt among debt-bound devils

Caelan fulfils the Free Ledger hearing when owed, exposes collar exit terms, shields public refusal, and creates room for named workers to choose. Sira wants immediate action, Oren wants families moved first, and Pellan fears losing lawful shelter. They decide separately. The route gains numbers, service tunnels, disruption, and moral legitimacy. It risks worker casualties, unstable streets, and loss of formal access. Its Elian channel comes from workers opening the engine's witness line.

### Win the invasion auction

Caelan audits only obligations legally owned and transferable. Strong evidence, the completed petition hearing, cooperative Compact surety, and exact witnesses create different bids. A weak save may use Caelan's own limited future appearance duty. The route gains lawful engine access, denies Sableglass the invasion right, and preserves civic stability. It restricts Caelan's future choices and may strengthen the Price Court. Its Elian channel comes from the engine-audit phrase inside the purchased access deed.

### March through the city by force

Caelan uses only the expedition that crossed. Roster preparations determine doors, retreat paths, formation, and injuries. This route reaches the engine fastest and avoids selling obligations. It spends Health, Command, supplies, or political standing and leaves damaged public streets. Hostile Vexa provides only truce-required warnings. Its Elian channel comes from a captured Sableglass route relay aligned with the fragment.

## Relationship illusion rules

Source priority is exact and physical: a committed crossed Mara, a committed crossed Lysara, an eligible explored or interested Vexa, then a non-romantic private future. An absent partner may appear only as a visibly false memory image. The illusion cannot speak new knowledge, create consent, change the real person's promises, or make a relationship choice for them.

Mara's image offers a quiet roadside home and asks Caelan to abandon everyone outside it. Lysara's offers a road beyond both kingdoms and asks him to place her people above every other claimant. Vexa's offers a private neutral house between realms and asks him to surrender the public witness line. The non-romantic image offers every survivor safely home and asks him to stop before the engine.

If Chapter Ten recorded mutual limits, those exact refused promises expose the imitation and create a free clear refusal. Public-only limits create a complete platonic answer. Caelan may affirm the wanted future while refusing its dishonest price. Denial, affirmation, and platonic refusal remain viable and do not reset commitment. No intimacy scene occurs in Chapter Eleven.

## Endings and Chapter Twelve handoff

All endings occur at the inner face of the Black Gate as it begins to open. They are final nodes until Chapter Twelve exists.

- `c11-ending-revolt`: debt-bound refusers hold the witness square and a Free Ledger faction offers help for a public renewal hearing. Cost: civic instability and any named casualty or damaged route.
- `c11-ending-auction`: Caelan holds the invasion right under its exact registered limits and a Price Court dissenting seat offers help for the right to review the replacement Gate law. Cost: transferred obligations and Caelan's named future restriction.
- `c11-ending-force`: the exact expedition holds the engine corridor and the Ash Compact offers help for one witnessed Gate passage after the crisis. Cost: physical damage, political hostility, or depleted command and supplies.

Every ending carries into Chapter Twelve:

- `c11-route-revolt`, `c11-route-auction`, or `c11-route-force`;
- the exact expedition roster and partner crossing flags;
- fragment custody, visibility, true-name scope, and return promise;
- every surviving, destroyed, released, and mythically restricted Oath;
- exact mythic freedom restriction and duration;
- Free Ledger hearing result and faction alliance;
- invasion-right holder and registered force limit;
- Vexa relationship and truce state;
- current resources, injuries, supplies, and route damage;
- `c11-elian-voice-heard` with only the bounded claims heard here;
- `c11-inner-gate-opening` and the unresolved question of a replacement promise.

Concrete Chapter Twelve consumers:

1. Revolt support can staff a witness circle but cannot impose a Gate law on nonparticipants.
2. Auction ownership can delay one registered invasion, while its review obligation limits Caelan's ability to refuse the Price Court.
3. Force control secures the corridor but increases the cost of winning voluntary devil consent.
4. Each mythic freedom restriction removes the matching Chapter Twelve action until its success condition is met or the player accepts a visible breach.
5. The fragment return promise becomes due only after Malrec's inside opening is stopped. Chapter Twelve must present neutral return as a choice or owned duty, not assume completion.
6. Elian's voice may support investigation but cannot be treated as proof of identity, death, singularity, or physical location.

## Representative walkthroughs

### High-resource revolt

Futureless witnesses use the accepted guide checkpoint. Caelan holds the owed hearing before the petition reaches the Court. Public exposure proves Sableglass debt fraud. The surviving Red Moot Oath receives Caelan's right to command beyond accepted limits for 2 Oathfire, creating a protected refusal square. Named workers choose rebellion after moving families. Their witness phrase opens the engine channel. Elian's bounded warning is heard, and the refusers reach the inner Gate with Free Ledger support.

### Low-resource auction

The guide was refused, so the expedition takes the slower public street and owes no hearing. Private seals remain closed. Caelan refuses mythic use and inventories a baseline appearance obligation plus a lawful public passage credit. Exact witnesses prove the bid without exposing private desires. He wins a narrow invasion right carrying a future Price Court review, audits the engine, hears Elian, and reaches the Gate without negative resources.

### Pell force route

Pell's map opens a service door while his escort protects his injured side. No running action is offered to him. If Medicine exists, treatment preserves the fastest retreat. Without it, the group uses his marked slow lane. The captured route relay aligns through the fragment, earns the engine phrase, and opens Elian's channel. The exact escort reaches the inner Gate.

### Mara relationship route

Mara crossed and remains committed. Her illusion offers a quiet home. Chapter Ten's exact mutual limits let Caelan affirm that he wants the home while refusing to abandon those outside it. The real Mara later recognises the respected limit, improving trust but granting no tactical superiority or new consent.

### Lysara relationship route

Lysara crossed as an independent envoy. Her illusion offers one road beyond both kingdoms for the price of placing Thornweald first forever. Her refusal to promise obedience exposes the imitation. Caelan can affirm the shared road and reject exclusive duty. No absent Ilyra or outside partner acts.

### Hostile Vexa force route

Vexa maintains armed public distance and provides only the threat warning required by the truce. Compact surety, private help, and romantic temptation are unavailable. Teren and six individual volunteers or the mixed company breach only named doors. Caelan pays physical or political costs, obtains the captured relay phrase, hears Elian once, and reaches the inner Gate.

### Non-romantic route

No eligible partner crossed. The illusion shows every traveller safely home and offers Caelan release from duty if he abandons the engine. Public-only limits or a plain refusal break it. The route remains complete without relationship reward, intimacy, or inferred consent.
