# Chapter Seven Map: The Red Wind Hunt

## Player promise

The chapter answers one main question: why is Asterra's own army hunting Caelan instead of defending the Black Gate?

The answer arrives in two clear parts. Regent Malrec used his lawful authority during the Queen's illness to move the Crown March away from four Gate forts. A separate hidden power uses the dead Marshal Evren's field signals to keep that army fighting in the west. The two enemies help each other, but they are not presented as the same person or power.

The immediate adventure remains physical: keep the Crown March away from Kharad Vey, survive a cavalry pursuit, stop loyal soldiers from killing one another, and turn the surviving force toward the Black Gate fort ring.

## Continuity entering the chapter

- The Red Moot chose war, a guarded alliance, or neutrality. This changes the physical battlefield, not merely the number of Kharad people mentioned.
- On the war route, the wheel town is present and its decks and families are at risk.
- On the alliance route, Korran's volunteers and two portable shield engines fight while the town remains several miles south.
- On the neutral route, Korran is one of three guides and Caelan draws the army away while the town continues south.
- Caelan carries Vaor's ember by gift, force, or pact. Each outcome remains visible in the opening and ending.
- Most Crown soldiers believe Caelan murdered Hale, stole a royal weapon, and forced Kharad Vey into rebellion.
- Regent Malrec has real legal authority while the Queen is ill. The army is not foreign and is not knowingly fighting the Queen.
- Mara, Lysara, and Ilyra retain their individual relationship intent.
- Rook is travelling through the Underways and never meets Ilyra in this chapter. Lio provides living Crown knowledge, while Korran owns the steppe signals.

## Fifteen decision structure

1. Protect the families, wounded people, and support crews present on the chosen Red Moot route.
2. Break the first cavalry line without treating loyal soldiers as willing villains.
3. Decide whether Lieutenant Lio Var remains a prisoner, returns to his army, joins Caelan, or serves as a guarded witness.
4. Choose which part of Malrec's genuine orders to preserve and spread.
5. Save people, proof, or time during the first full attack.
6. Stop the dead marshal from using the signal horn available on the current route.
7. Help Ilyra trace the hidden command toward the Black Gate.
8. Resolve or deepen the personal relationship most relevant to Caelan's current path.
9. Confront Marshal Teren Voss with one earned reason to doubt Malrec.
10. Survive Evren's attempt to seize the army from its living marshal.
11. Prepare the wounded, evidence, terrain, or one verified living signal post.
12. Choose the defining plan: salt trap, public exposure, or steppe duel.
13. Execute the chosen plan through Caelan's class strengths or a stored advantage.
14. Choose between saving many, saving one, burning proof, accepting Lio's lasting legal risk when he joined, or accepting Teren's injury.
15. Take the full army, a chosen company, or no formal new allies.

Every complete route contains exactly fifteen decisions.

## The Crown March

The Crown March is an Asterra field army. The soldiers are serving their own kingdom, not invading it from another country. They obey Regent Malrec because he governs in the ill Queen's name. Their sealed orders are legally issued but built on false charges.

The player is given reasons not to slaughter them:

- most have never met Caelan or seen Dragonspine;
- the army claims Caelan murdered Hale, although Caelan last saw Hale alive as the Dragonspine grave began to collapse;
- their living marshal, Teren, values the Crown above the Regent personally;
- soldiers can change sides only after evidence, law, or witnessed restraint gives them a defensible choice.

## The two commands

Malrec's order is living and legal. It moved the Crown March away from four Gate forts three weeks before Caelan took the ember.

Evren's order has no paper, wax, living messenger, or today's password. It uses the authentic habits of a marshal dead for nineteen years. The hidden sender beyond the Gate uses this dead command to hold the army west.

The chapter never requires the player to understand how the Unsea works. The useful test is simple:

1. Where is the paper?
2. Where is the living messenger?
3. What is today's password?

The explanation order is fixed. The opening names the Crown March and the people in danger. Lio then names Evren, Teren, and dead command. The field map gives Malrec one test, the date on his paper, and Evren another test, today's password. Hale's fate is always described from Caelan's last confirmed sighting before the Crown's murder claim is repeated.

During active battle prose, Crown army is the default short name. Crown March remains the formal name used in objectives, political decisions, and ending states.

## Personal scene routing

The eighth decision follows the player's established relationship rather than forcing Mara into every route.

### Mara

Mara asks what Caelan is willing to live for. The player may commit to a shared future, place duty first with honest limits, end the romance, or choose friendship.

### Lysara

Lysara asks whether partnership can survive two kingdoms and political disagreement. The player may commit without demanding obedience, delay commitment, end the romance, or choose friendship.

### Ilyra

An unattached Caelan who acknowledged interest may deepen the bond, slow it down, choose a platonic alliance, or reject intimacy used as leverage. Threadread never substitutes for spoken consent.

### No active romance

Caelan prepares with his companions. The scene rewards trust, planning, local knowledge, or rehearsing why a dead command cannot answer today's password without treating romance as required content.

## Lio location contract

- A prisoner stays bound and may give limited testimony, but is never called a deserter.
- A released Lio stays inside the Crown ranks. Only messages, flags, and effects that can travel to him may use his help.
- A recruited Lio may act beside Caelan and may become a deserter through a public choice.
- A guarded witness remains present without becoming a volunteer.
- Later menus must use route-specific options so Lio never appears where the player did not leave him.

## Defining routes

### Salt Basin trap

Caelan uses Kharad terrain to stop the army without drowning it. The winning image is a trap with a genuine path of surrender. The army may respect his mercy while resenting the humiliation.

### Order exposure

Caelan carries Malrec's dates and seals through the ranks. Soldiers turn through their own judgment. This route produces the strongest political challenge to the Regent while placing people and documents under direct attack.

### Steppe duel

Korran explains the open ground duel rule during the parley, and Teren accepts it aloud. Each leader may stake one order before the witnesses. No magic forces Crown obedience, and the duel does not grant permanent command. The goal is not to kill Teren.

## Command and Guard pressure

The final storm creates Caelan's class conflict in plain form:

- Command moves hundreds to safety while one companion suffers a lasting injury.
- Direct intervention costs Health and saves the companion while a broken company suffers losses.
- Oathfire saves both groups by burning the original Gate orders, destroying the best legal evidence against Malrec.
- Lio can make the single rescue only if he joined Caelan, but he loses his Crown badge and becomes a named deserter among uncertain soldiers.
- Teren and two engineers can perform the rescue on other routes, leaving Teren injured and his army under shared living command.

There is no option that preserves every person, proof, resource, and future advantage.

## End states

### Full Crown March

Caelan gains the largest force and can attempt to defend all eight forts. He also inherits divided loyalty and thousands of soldiers trained to obey before questioning.

### Chosen company

Volunteers ride ahead with Caelan while Teren brings the rest east on a separate road under his own command. This is a smaller but more stable force for the player without leaving the Gate undefended.

### Dangerous reputation

Caelan refuses formal command. Teren leads the Crown March east on a separate road and sends copies of the evidence toward Greyhaven. Caelan's existing companions reach the Gate first, so he gains no new formal military support in Chapter Eight's opening.

Every ending grants equal Wayfire. The difference is force size, loyalty, speed, political reach, and risk.

## Continuity rules for Chapter Eight

- Malrec deliberately emptied at least four Black Gate forts before the Dragonspine crisis.
- Four forts were emptied by Malrec's order, three more signal fires later went cold, and smoke still rises from occupied Fourth Fort beneath an upside down banner.
- A hidden will beyond the Gate used Marshal Evren's dead voice.
- The method used to trace that will determines what it learned about Caelan, Vaor's ember, Lio, or Oathfire.
- Teren survives every route and rejects the dead command.
- If Caelan takes volunteers or refuses command, Teren still leads the remaining Crown March east on a separate road.
- The original Gate orders may survive, be copied widely, or burn in the final rescue.
- One companion may carry a lasting injury, a Crown company may have suffered losses, or Lio may have lost his Crown status during the rescue.
- Caelan arrives with a full army, a chosen company, or only his established allies and reputation.
- Ilyra leaves to follow the hidden thread by another route while retaining her relationship state.
- The chapter ends when something knocks from the other side of the Black Gate.
