# Player Revelation Ledger

## How to use this ledger

This is the author facing control sheet for player understanding. Every chapter must preserve the distinction between what the author knows and what the player has seen proved.

The answer closes the active mystery. The next hook supplies anticipation. The mechanic is the only new system idea that may need teaching in that chapter.

# Caelan: The Ember Oath

## 1. The Road Before Rain

Active question: Was the sealed route deliberately changed, and did the attackers know how Caelan might respond?

Answer: The attackers received plans for every route before Caelan chose. The sealed change was deliberate, but its maker remains unknown.

Next hook: How can the same road lead to the wrong coast, and why was Ordan listed among the few officials who knew the escort date?

Mechanic: Health, Guard, and Command through safe escort work.

## 2. The Inn That Waited

Active question: Who prepared the siege at Bellweather Inn, and how will the survivors stop it?

Answer: Ordan used a forged warning and a loosened road pin to create a secret route for Crown soldiers. He needed Caelan's road authority and Lysara's living seed to open its old locks. Driving the pin home closes it.

Next hook: Can Caelan reach Harrowfen before Ordan uses the remaining fragment?

Mechanic: Medicine, siege priorities, and the immediate burden of a minor Oath.

## 3. The Town at the Wrong Mile

Active question: Can Caelan clear his name and catch Ordan before the courier escapes Harrowfen?

Answer: The fragment pulls distant roads together and belongs to the larger World Nail that keeps distance stable. Ordan forged the later evidence, but admits the impossible sealed order came from someone above him.

Next hook: Why does Rook steal the fragment from Ordan on the Mileless Bridge?

Mechanic: Low resource leadership during a manhunt, with visible rescue and pursuit tradeoffs.

## 4. Thief at the Mileless Bridge

Active question: Can Caelan recover the fragment and save the escort?

Answer: The fragment is part of a map showing several Nail sites.

Next hook: Why is the Crown moving it toward Dragonspine?

Mechanic: Combined class solutions during a crossover.

## 5. The Dragon's Cold Grave

Active question: What waits at the fire mark on the map?

Answer: A dragon guards evidence that the Concord was created through sacrifice.

Next hook: What was cut away when the world became stable?

Mechanic: Oathfire and a dragon pact, demonstrated before a major vow.

## 6. The City on Wheels

Active question: Will the orc clans help carry the ember?

Answer: They will help only if the future order recognises their living authority.

Next hook: Are the voices in their storms truly dead ancestors?

Mechanic: Reputation earned through local service.

## 7. The Red Wind Hunt

Active question: Who is controlling the pursuing army?

Answer: Malrec deliberately emptied the Black Gate's defence.

Next hook: What is he preparing to release?

Mechanic: Command at large scale and mutually exclusive protection.

## 8. Eight Empty Forts

Active question: Why were the forts abandoned?

Answer: Soldiers sold future promises to devils during hidden Gate openings.

Next hook: What do the devils want from Caelan now?

Mechanic: Several active Oaths tested in one visible ledger.

## 9. The Price of a Name

Active question: Can Caelan recover the other Gate fragment?

Answer: Vexa has it, Malrec has allies inside the Deep, and exact promises govern passage.

Next hook: Can Caelan survive a realm where every desire has a price?

Mechanic: Contract wording through bounded negotiation.

## 10. The Ash Road

Active question: How do travellers remain free in the Cinder Deep?

Next hook: Which faction controls the city and Malrec's engine?

Mechanic: Small reversible bargains before dangerous contracts.

## 11. City of Every Price

Active question: Why is Malrec willing to merge the realms?

Answer: He is trying to recover Elian, who asks not to be restored this way.

Next hook: What new promise can replace the failing Gate law?

Mechanic: Mythic Oathfire with accumulated costs.

## 12. The Ember Oath

Active question: What should the relationship between Edrath and the Deep become?

Answer: The player's Gate decision answers this and permanently changes dawn.

Next hook: What will the changed dawn allow someone else to do?

Mechanic: Finale synthesis with no new mechanic.

# Standalone play rule

A player beginning with Rook or Ilyra receives a short world state card containing only facts the new protagonist can observe. The card names no more than three consequences and never summarises another protagonist's emotional ending.

Every essential answer in this ledger can still be learned inside the active series. Playing earlier series adds recognition, relationships, and consequence, not basic comprehension.
