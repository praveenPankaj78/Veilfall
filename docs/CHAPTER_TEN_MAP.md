# Chapter Ten Map

## Identity

Title: The Ash Road

Playable hero: Caelan Vey

Canonical entry: `c10-ash-road`

Location: the road from the inner Black Gate to Vathis in the Cinder Deep.

Immediate objective: bring the exact voluntary expedition to Vathis without letting an automatic offer bind any traveller.

Active question: How can travellers remain free in a realm where spoken wants automatically become offers?

Chapter answer: freedom requires time and visible consent between desire and acceptance. The company can create that time through shared offers, protected private choices, or a precisely limited Oath accepted by every affected traveller.

Next hook: Which faction controls Vathis and Malrec's engine?

The chapter does not reveal Elian, a daughter, or the reason Malrec wants the realms joined.

## Offer and acceptance rules

The first offer uses a cup of water. Ash folds into a clay cup after someone says they want a drink. Written marks state the benefit, price, duration, accepting action, and exit condition.

The demonstration keeps these states separate:

1. Desire causes an offer to appear.
2. The offer owns nothing while it waits.
3. Silence accepts nothing.
4. Clear speech or the exact accepting action begins the bargain.
5. The benefit, price, duration, and exit condition are visible first.
6. Returning the empty cup to the marked stone ends the reversible water bargain.
7. The completed bargain cannot add a later price.
8. True-name knowledge points only to the witnessed Chapter Nine fragment agreement.
9. Attraction, curiosity, fear, and magical knowledge never count as acceptance or intimacy consent.

If Caelan learned the desire-offer danger in Chapter Nine, he prepares the company before anyone speaks a want. Every traveller receives a pause phrase and gains a free coordinated refusal during the first offer.

If he did not learn it, the cup appears before the explanation. The route remains completable, but one traveller must hold position while Vexa demonstrates the exit condition.

4. Sableglass listening ash tries to compare private answers and split the company.

## Defining routes

### Share every offer

Every traveller speaks the benefit and price of each offer before anyone answers. The method buys coordination and exposes Sableglass substitutions immediately.

Advantage: the company can protect one another from hidden wording and earns the Free Ledger's public route seal.

Cost: painful desires become public inside the expedition. A partner, witness, or officer loses privacy and may gain Friction even while the group becomes safer.

### Allow private negotiation

Each traveller receives an opaque seal, two exits, and time alone with a chosen witness outside hearing range. Only acceptance status and danger are reported to the group.

Advantage: private desires remain private, and each traveller retains direct control over any bargain.

Cost: Sableglass can create one false emergency because the group cannot compare full terms. The company must spend position, time, or a route resource to reunite.

Privacy is treated as a real freedom rather than a mistake. With the Chapter Nine warning and strong evidence preparation, the false emergency can be identified without opening anyone's seal.

### Take the temptations through a new Oath

Every affected traveller hears the complete Oath and must explicitly agree. Any refusal removes that person from its scope without removing them from the company.

Exact wording: “With each named traveller's permission, I will carry the Ash Road's offers until we enter Vathis or that traveller withdraws. I will speak each offer before answering it. If I accept for another person or hide an offer, this Oath breaks and every carried offer returns to me at once.”

Scope: only named consenting travellers and only offers created on the Ash Road.

Duration: until Vathis entry or individual withdrawal.

Success condition: reach Vathis without accepting or hiding another person's offer.

Breach condition: Caelan accepts for someone else or conceals a carried offer.

Exit condition: any traveller may withdraw by speaking their own name and taking back only their offer.

Immediate advantage: private offers become visible ash bands on Caelan's shield, leaving companions free to watch the road and move together.

Later cost: each carried temptation presses against Caelan's existing Oaths, costs Oathfire or Resolve during the road danger, and becomes a Chapter Eleven breach risk until the success condition is complete.

## Relationships and intimacy

The central limits conversation uses only a person physically present.

- Mara refuses to promise that she will wait safely behind while Caelan carries every danger. Caelan refuses to promise that he will choose her life over every traveller in his care.
- Lysara refuses to promise obedience to a Crown future. Caelan refuses to promise sole elven control of the Gate.

- With no romantic partner, Ansel, Pell, or Teren refuses unlimited command, and Caelan refuses to promise that leadership removes personal choice.

Respecting both limits changes a later offer and increases Trust or Respect. Trying to turn a limit into proof of love increases Friction and strengthens the matching temptation.

It occurs after the road danger and every active offer have ended, with no bargain underway, no magic in the shelter, and two open exits. Existing intimacy creates no presumption. Both adults answer again and may choose conversation or separate rest.

## Free Ledger bargain

The Free Ledger offers a marked guide road from the final Ash Road shelter to Vathis's public gate.

Benefit: a guide walks the company past one faction checkpoint and identifies the engine district from outside.

Price: the expedition carries the Free Ledger's sealed petition to Vathis and gives its named signers one public hearing before handing the petition to any ruling faction.

Duration: from acceptance until the petition receives that hearing.

Accepting action: Caelan and one expedition witness press separate thumb marks beside the full terms.

Exit condition: either side may withdraw before the Vathis gate. Withdrawal closes the marked guide road and leaves neither side owing the petition hearing.

The completed guide bargain cannot widen into military service, loyalty, true-name access, intimacy, or control of the Gate fragment.

Refusal remains playable. The company reaches Vathis by the slower public road with the same core revelation but without a devil guide or faction ally.
